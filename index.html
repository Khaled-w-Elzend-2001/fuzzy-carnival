About me

My story

I am Samar, a daughter of Algeria, one-and-twenty years of age, with a mind drawn to the order of engineering and a heart devoted to the freedom of art. I seek to understand how things are made, yet linger just as long upon the beauty of their smallest details.

Through study, I learn to give my ideas structure; through drawing, I give them life. My pencil hath become a familiar companion, tracing light, shaping shadows, and lending form to all that words leave unspoken. Between the measured line and the imagined world, my story continueth to unfold.

My passion

My soul hath ever found its home among the quiet wonders of this world—the hush of winter, the whisper of leaves, and the sea that speaketh when all else is still.

Oft have I sat beside the window, a cup of tea warming my hands, whilst thoughts too gentle for words awaited their life upon the page. There, beneath the patient touch of my pencil, what dwelleth unseen within my heart taketh shape at last.

For art is how I hold what time will not permit to linger—a little tenderness, a passing light, the peace of a creature sleeping near. And if a soul should pause before my work and feel, for but a moment, less alone, then these quiet hands shall have spoken well.
